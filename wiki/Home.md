# Language Cards Wiki

Welcome to the **Language Cards** wiki. Language Cards is a flashcard app for learning vocabulary in English ↔ French, English ↔ Spanish, and English ↔ German. It is built with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform, and runs on Android and iOS.

## Features

- **Bidirectional practice** — Practice in both directions (English → Foreign and Foreign → English)
- **Hit/miss tracking** — Per card and per direction, to prioritize difficult cards
- **Session summary** — See accuracy and progress after each practice round
- **Overall stats** — Track total hits, misses, and accuracy across all sessions
- **Deck management** — Add, delete, and restore decks; import custom decks from JSON (Android)
- **Built-in decks** — French (Basics, Past Tense, Conversation); Spanish (Basics, Conversation); German (Basics, Conversation)
